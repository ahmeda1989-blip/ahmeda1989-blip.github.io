4. Statistics and crash reports

Apple and Google provide us, as the app provider, with statistics, e.g. on the number of downloads and purchases and on the device and operating system versions used. These statistics are aggregated; we cannot identify individual persons from them.

If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics or diagnostic data with app developers (iPhone/iPad: “Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements”; Android: “Usage & diagnostics”), we also receive crash reports through Apple or Google. These contain technical information such as the device model, operating system version and the time and cause of the crash, but no names or content you entered in the app. We use this information solely to fix bugs and improve our apps. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in apps that work correctly). You can turn off this sharing in your device settings at any time.

5. Device backups

Our apps store their data only on your device. If you have enabled backups in your device settings (e.g. iCloud Backup on iPhone/iPad or Google backup on Android), this app data – like the data of other apps – is part of that backup, so that you can restore it on a new device, for example. Backups are stored by Apple or Google in your account under their own responsibility; we have no access to them. You decide in your device settings whether backups are made.

Apple or Google also provide us with feedback that you send yourself (e.g. text and screenshots), as well as crash reports and information on the use of the test version (e.g. installations and device model). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the test at your request). You can leave the test at any time; we remove you from the tester list as soon as you no longer wish to take part or the test has ended.

We delete the message once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail); this may involve a transfer to the USA.
